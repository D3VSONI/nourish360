Build "Nourish360", a production-grade, offline-first, DPDP Act 2023-compliant maternal, postpartum mother, and child health triage, longitudinal risk forecasting, and Blinkit-style emergency ambulance dispatch platform for rural India.

### 1. CORE ARCHITECTURE & TECH STACK
- **Frontend:** React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS + Lucide Icons + Leaflet (`react-leaflet` with OpenStreetMap tiles for live Blinkit-style ambulance map tracking) + Recharts (for longitudinal vitals trend charts).
- **Backend:** Node.js + Express + TypeScript + Replit's built-in PostgreSQL (`DATABASE_URL`, Drizzle ORM or `pg`). Auto-create tables and seed realistic rural Haryana (Sonipat district) data on server startup.
- **OFFLINE-FIRST RESILIENCE:** Mirror patient records, active assessments, and triage rules in client-side `IndexedDB` / `localStorage` with an automatic background sync queue (`POST /api/sync`) so clinical triage, 6-month risk projection, and emergency stabilization checklists work even in Offline / 2G mode, syncing seamlessly to PostgreSQL when online.

### 2. DPDP ACT 2023 PRIVACY & SECURITY ARCHITECTURE (STRICTLY ENFORCED)
Implement real Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 safeguards in the backend and database:
1. **Verifiable Consent Ledger (`dpdp_consent_logs` table):** Store `user_id`, `patient_type`, `guardian_name` (mandatory if `patient_type === 'CHILD'`), `consent_purpose` (`"CLINICAL_TRIAGE_AND_EMERGENCY_REFERRAL"`), `consent_version`, `ip_or_device_hash`, `granted_at`, and `revoked_at`. Block profile creation unless explicit consent is checked.
2. **Data Minimization & PII Protection:** Store only minimum-necessary clinical fields. Mask phone numbers in UI views (`+91-98XXXX3210`), use HTTP-only JWT session tokens after OTP verification, and log every data access/update in an immutable `audit_logs` table.
3. **Data Principal Rights API:** Provide `GET /api/privacy/export/:healthId` (JSON health record download) and `DELETE /api/privacy/erase/:healthId` (right to erasure / anonymization) accessible from a subtle "DPDP Privacy Controls" drawer.

### 3. POSTGRESQL DATABASE SCHEMA
Create and auto-seed these 6 tables:
1. `users`: `id` (UUID), `health_id` (unique string e.g. `N360-SNP-4821`), `patient_type` (`CHILD` | `PREGNANT_LADY` | `MOTHER`), `full_name`, `phone_number`, `village`, `guardian_name` (required for `CHILD`), `role_specific_profile` (JSONB: for `CHILD` -> `{dob_months, birth_weight_kg, immunization_status}`; for `PREGNANT_LADY` -> `{age, lmp_date, gestational_weeks, gravida, blood_group}`; for `MOTHER` -> `{age, delivery_date, days_postpartum, delivery_type, blood_group}`), `created_at`.
2. `dpdp_consent_logs`: `id`, `health_id`, `guardian_consent` (boolean), `purpose`, `granted_at`.
3. `clinical_visits`: `id`, `health_id`, `patient_type`, `vitals` (JSONB: `bp_sys`, `bp_dia`, `hb`, `weight_kg`, `temp_c`, `resp_rate`, `muac_cm`), `symptoms` (JSONB array), `risk_tier` (`GREEN` | `AMBER` | `RED`), `triggered_rules` (JSONB array), `six_month_forecast` (JSONB array of `{condition, probability_level, timeline, clinical_reason, preventive_action}`), `routed_facility` (JSONB), `created_at`.
4. `facilities`: `id`, `name`, `tier`, `lat`, `lng`, `distance_km`, `capabilities` (JSONB array), `blood_bank_status` (`AVAILABLE` | `OUT_OF_STOCK` | `NONE`), `nicus_available` (integer).
5. `ambulance_bookings`: `id`, `booking_code`, `health_id` (nullable for unauthenticated emergency), `patient_type` (`CHILD` | `PREGNANT_LADY` | `MOTHER`), `emergency_type`, `pickup_village`, `pickup_lat`, `pickup_lng`, `destination_facility_id`, `destination_facility_name`, `bypassed_facilities` (JSONB), `driver_name`, `driver_phone`, `ambulance_number`, `eta_seconds`, `status` (`DISPATCHED` | `EN_ROUTE` | `ARRIVED`), `created_at`.
6. `audit_logs`: `id`, `health_id`, `action`, `actor`, `timestamp`.

### 4. EXACT 4-PAGE USER FLOW & BACKEND ENDPOINTS

#### PAGE 1: TRI-CATEGORY ENTRY & BLINKIT-STYLE 108 AMBULANCE TRACKER
- **Main Screen:**
  - Clean header (`Notion360` + Online/Offline Sync Badge + DPDP Shield Badge).
  - **Top-Right High-Contrast Emergency Button:** `🚨 BOOK 108 AMBULANCE (EMERGENCY)` — accessible in 1 tap without login.
  - **Three Large Role Selection Cards:**
    1. **Child (0–5 Years)** — Growth, Nutrition & Pediatric Triage
    2. **Pregnant Lady** — Antenatal Care (ANC) & High-Risk Pregnancy Triage
    3. **Mother (Postpartum & Lactating)** — Post-Delivery Recovery & Maternal Care
- **Emergency Ambulance Booking & Blinkit-Style Live Tracking Flow (`POST /api/ambulance/book` & `GET /api/ambulance/:id`):**
  - Clicking the corner Ambulance button opens a fast 10-second Emergency Dispatch Sheet: select patient category (`Pregnant Lady` | `Mother` | `Child`), emergency trigger (e.g., *Excessive Blood Loss / Hemorrhage*, *Seizures / High BP*, *High Fever / Breathing Difficulty*, *Labor Pain*), and pickup village (with GPS/preset coordinates).
  - **Zero-Bounce Capability Routing:** Backend automatically routes the ambulance past unequipped clinics (e.g., bypassing *PHC Ganaur* if no Blood Bank/NICU) directly to the nearest capable FRU (*District Civil Hospital, Sonipat*), displaying why closer clinics were bypassed.
  - **Blinkit-Style Live Tracking View:** Once booked, immediately transition to a split live-tracking view:
    - **Top Half (Live Map & ETA):** Interactive Leaflet map showing animated ambulance marker moving along a route polyline toward the patient's village pin, live countdown timer (`Arriving in 08 mins 42 secs`), ambulance registration (`HR-10-GV-1084`), driver name, and a **"Trigger Automated Family Voice Call"** button.
    - **Bottom Half (Interactive Golden-Hour Stabilization Guide — Dynamic by Patient Type):** Displays clear, step-by-step visual emergency precautions and body positioning while waiting for the ambulance:
      - *If Pregnant Lady:* **Left-Lateral Tilt Position** (lie on left side to prevent vena cava compression), elevate legs if excessive blood loss/shock, do NOT give oral food/water if drowsy/seizing, keep airway clear.
      - *If Postpartum Mother:* **Uterine Fundal Massage & Shock Position** (lie flat with legs elevated 12 inches for excessive blood loss/PPH), keep warm with blankets, monitor consciousness every 2 minutes.
      - *If Child:* **Kangaroo Mother Care (Skin-to-Skin Warming)** for hypothermia, **Recovery / Side-Lying Position** if vomiting/convulsing, clear nasal airway gently, maintain small frequent sips/breastfeeding if alert.

#### PAGE 2: ROLE-SPECIFIC INTAKE, DPDP CONSENT & SMS/CALL OTP AUTH
- Selecting **Child**, **Pregnant Lady**, or **Mother** on Page 1 opens Page 2 with two clean tabs: **New Registration (Generate ID)** and **Returning User Login (Health ID + Phone OTP)**.
- **Minimal, Role-Tailored Registration Fields (`POST /api/auth/register`):**
  - *Common:* Full Name, Mobile Number, Village/Block, and **Mandatory DPDP Act 2023 Consent Checkbox** (plus Guardian Name & Guardian Consent toggle when `Child` is selected).
  - *If Child:* Age (in months), Birth Weight (kg), Last Recorded Weight (kg).
  - *If Pregnant Lady:* Age, Gestational Weeks (or LMP), Blood Group, Gravida (pregnancy count).
  - *If Mother:* Age, Days Since Delivery, Delivery Type (Normal / C-Section), Blood Group.
- **Unique Health ID & SMS/Call Verification (`POST /api/auth/send-otp`, `POST /api/auth/verify-otp`):**
  - Registration generates a unique ID (`N360-SNP-XXXX`), logs DPDP consent in PostgreSQL, and dispatches the ID + 6-digit OTP via Twilio SMS or Automated Voice Call (`twiml` voice readout in Hindi/English for low-literacy users).
  - Returning users enter their `Notion360 Health ID` + `Phone Number`, choose **Verify via SMS** or **Verify via Voice Call**, and log in to load their past longitudinal visit history. *(Include a clean Demo OTP helper toast if Twilio env vars are empty).*

#### PAGE 3: ROLE-SPECIFIC SYMPTOM & VITALS ASSESSMENT
- Dynamically renders **only** the clinical conditions, danger signs, and vitals relevant to the selected user type:
  - **For Children (IMNCI & Nutrition):** Fast/difficult breathing (Resp Rate), High fever or cold body (`Temp °C`), Unable to drink/breastfeed, Vomiting everything, Lethargy/unconsciousness, Umbilical redness/pus, Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (`MUAC cm`) / Current Weight (`kg`).
  - **For Pregnant Ladies (PMSMA):** Systolic & Diastolic BP, Hemoglobin (`Hb g/dL`), Current Weight (`kg`), Severe headache, Blurred vision / flashes, Sudden swelling of face/hands, Vaginal bleeding, Reduced/absent fetal movement, Severe abdominal pain.
  - **For Mothers (Postpartum PPH & Sepsis):** Excessive vaginal blood loss (soaking >1 pad/hour), High fever/chills, Foul-smelling discharge, Severe dizziness/fainting, BP, Hemoglobin (`Hb g/dL`), Breast engorgement/pain.

#### PAGE 4: LONGITUDINAL ANALYSIS, 3-TIER RISK ENGINE & 6-MONTH FORECAST
- Submitting Page 3 (`POST /api/visits/analyze`) runs the deterministic clinical engine comparing **current visit inputs** against **all past stored visits** for that `health_id`, then renders Page 4 divided into 3 unmistakable clinical zones:
  - **1. Current Status Banner (`GREEN` / `AMBER` / `RED`):**
    - **GREEN (Normal):** *"Everything is normal. Current vitals and historical trends are within safe Indian clinical ranges."* -> Displays routine ANC/PNC/Immunization next-visit schedule.
    - **AMBER (Concerning — Needs Clinical Review):** *"Things are concerning and must be reviewed."* -> Highlights exact subtle anomalies (e.g., BP jumped `+18 mmHg` since last visit, mild anemia `Hb 9.2 g/dL`, or static child weight across 2 visits) + books priority PHC tele-review.
    - **RED (Critical — Immediate Action Required):** *"Critical danger signs detected. Immediate hospital intervention required."* -> Displays exact triggered PMSMA/IMNCI rules, triggers **Zero-Bounce Capability Routing** to the nearest equipped FRU, and provides a **1-Tap "Dispatch 108 Ambulance Now" button** that launches Page 1's Blinkit-style live tracking map with patient details pre-filled.
  - **2. Longitudinal Past-vs-Present Comparison Card:**
    - Recharts multi-visit trend graph + plain-language comparison showing whether the patient is improving, stable, or deteriorating compared to previous checkups.
  - **3. 6-Month Forward Risk Projection Card:**
    - Deterministic clinical trajectory forecast showing **what specific complications the patient could face in the next 6 months** if current trends continue (e.g., *Moderate Anemia (`Hb 8.8`) + Pregnancy -> High risk of Postpartum Hemorrhage & Low Birth Weight delivery within 3–5 months*; *Static Infant Weight -> High risk of Severe Acute Malnutrition / Stunting over next 6 months*), paired with concrete preventive nutrition/medical actions and a **Hindi Voice Readout (`window.speechSynthesis` `hi-IN`)** button.

### 5. SEED DATA FOR INSTANT DEMO
Seed 3 returning user accounts with rich 3-visit historical records (one `GREEN` Child, one `AMBER` Pregnant Lady with rising BP velocity, and one `RED` Postpartum Mother) plus a 1-click **"Demo Quick-Fill"** bar so judges can test both new registrations and returning longitudinal analyses in seconds.
